What would change the recommendation

  • A trial result that removes the hypothesised benefit.
  • A safety signal that removes the therapeutic index.
  • An access decision that makes the selected route unavailable.
  • A full-length AR structure that changes the target-confidence picture.
  • A named external reviewer finding the recommendation unsafe or unsupported.
