The pipeline

From target to cited dossier.

A complete computational pipeline: resolve the target, assemble the evidence, assess the structure, choose a mechanism and modality, design a candidate, run the safety gates, and return a citation-referenced dossier. The scientist sets the question; NEXUS organises the evidence.

Phase I · Resolve

Target and evidence

01

Intake & identity

Resolve a protein target, sequence, gene or disease against public reference data. Unknown identifiers fail loudly rather than being guessed.

02

Evidence assembly

Gather disease, gene, structure and trial evidence, with a source and an evidence tier attached to each claim.

03

Research record

Turn the question into a structured record: what is known, what is uncertain, what is missing, and what would count as an answer.

Phase II · Structure

Fold, assess, locate

04

Supervised structural recovery

Predict a structure and score it against an experimental reference. Guided results are labelled as reconstruction, not blind prediction.

05

Research folding path

A research path that attempts a fold without a template. It is reported separately and never presented as a production prediction.

06

Domains & pockets

Locate domains and binding cavities before any design begins, using experimental and predicted structural evidence.

Phase III · Decide

Gate, route, design

07

Mechanism gate

Gain of function may be degraded; loss of function is protected. The refusal is explicit: no degrader is issued where the mechanism would make the patient worse.

08

Modality routing

Choose among therapeutic routes — small molecule, biologic, gene therapy, editing, RNA-directed approach, protein replacement, cell therapy or symptomatic care.

09

Candidate design

Record the target-engagement hypothesis, the proposed readout, the safety questions and the falsifier. Designs are research hypotheses, not medicines.

10

Safety & developability

Citation, evidence, safety and scope gates run before release. Unsupported claims and clinical overreach are rejected by design.

Phase IV · Evidence

Assemble, match, verify

11

Knowledge assembly

Bring biomedical databases and registries into one evidence chain, with provenance and retrieval dates.

12

Patient & trial context

Where appropriate, rank recruiting trials against variant, therapy line, biomarkers and safety profile — as research context, not clinical advice.

13

Verify & report

Run the citation, evidence and compassion gates; then return a cited dossier with its limitations and scope note attached.

14

Dossier & review

The output is a cited dossier: disease, gene, evidence, hypotheses, candidate routes, trials, gaps and the next experiment. The scientist reviews and challenges it.

How it works

Four phases, fourteen stages.

Each phase has a defined input, a defined method and a defined output. The full stage map — every stage, the evidence layer and the measured output of the current dossiers — is on the pipeline page.

Phase I · Resolve the target

Identity first. The sequence, organism, length and mass are fetched from UniProt at run time; a deep ortholog alignment supplies the co-evolutionary signal; DCA and ContactNet produce two independent contact maps. Unknown accessions fail loudly rather than being guessed at.

Phase II · Solve the structure

Structure is solved by the supervised NIGHTINGALE path and, for research, by the ab initio research engine. Both report a TM-score (1.0 perfect, above 0.5 the same fold) and an RMSD in ångströms against the experimental reference. Guided numbers are labelled as reconstruction, not blind prediction; the unguided run is reported separately. Domains and binding pockets are located before any design begins.

Phase III · Decide and design

The gate reads mechanism before molecule. A gain-of-function target may be degraded; a loss-of-function target is protected, because removing a protein the patient already lacks would make them worse. The refusal is code — UBE3A in Angelman syndrome returns BLOCKED. Thirteen modality routes are available, and the designer computes target-binding ligand, linker and autophagy-recruiting ligand mass, logP and permeability. Two further validation stages run over the the canonical designs: a docking estimate from warhead IC50 (not a simulation) and a rule-based toxicity screen (structural alerts, not measured).

Phase IV · Evidence, patients, source checks

Twenty-four biomedical databases are surfaced through the knowledge layer, with live connectors to UniProt, PubChem, PDB, ClinicalTrials.gov, ChEMBL, NCBI Gene, Reactome and OpenTargets. The multi-dimensional matcher ranks recruiting trials against the patient's variant, therapy line, biomarkers and safety. The TJ Compassion audit and L.O.V.E. gate run before release, and the dossier is rendered to PDF natively — with its limitations declared.

Disease registry

A mechanistic registry, not one-size-fits-all.

Each target is routed by its mechanism — a channel disease needs a blocker, a trafficking defect needs a corrector, a toxic gain-of-function may need a degrader. The pipeline applies the right intervention to the right mechanism, and refuses the wrong one. NEXUS is disease-agnostic; this initial registry focuses on rare disease because the data is structured, not because the platform is limited to it.

Source checks

Source-checked and dated, or it doesn't ship.

Every factual claim — molecular weight, protein identifier, trial status, drug interaction — is checked against a live authoritative source before it is published. This is a design rule of the system, not a marketing promise.

Live cross-checking

UniProt, RCSB PDB, PubChem and ClinicalTrials.gov are queried live. Every record is checked against a named source, not assumed.

Citation audit

A built-in cite-audit flags any citation that does not belong to its claim — rather than letting it ship silently.

Honest labelling

When a fact cannot be checked, it is labelled unverified — never presented as certain. Unknown identifiers are rejected, not guessed.

The principle: a recommendation either ships with its source checks, or it does not ship at all. This is the difference between a system that claims accuracy and one that shows its work.

Scope: NEXUS NIGHTINGALE produces computational predictions and evidence dossiers for research use. It is not a clinical service, nothing here is medical advice, and no output has regulatory approval. The guided fold figure is a reconstruction baseline — the engine is directed toward the known structure — and the unguided figure is reported alongside it. Every benchmark on this page is reproducible with the shipped build.

Technology

The technology.

A sovereign, self-contained stack — no cloud dependency, no per-query cost, designed to run on commodity hardware.

Protein folding

A supervised folding engine that recovers near-native structure, and reports its own confidence against the reference — sub-ångström agreement on the benchmark target (1M17: TM 0.9801 / RMSD 0.94 Å on the clean build).

Molecule design

Computes designed candidates with explicit molecular weight, linker geometry and property checks — permeability, logP, blood-brain-barrier.

Knowledge engine

Twenty-four therapeutic databases surfaced into a single evidence chain, with every record carrying its citation.

Sovereign by design

Runs entirely on local hardware. No data leaves the machine, no external query costs, nothing dependent on a third-party service remaining available.
