Foundations and support organisations

If you found a Sabre-Tek dossier while looking for help, this page points to organisations that provide information and support. Sabre-Tek does not provide clinical advice, diagnosis, treatment or patient support.

Please read this first. Sabre-Tek is a research company. We publish source-linked research dossiers. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, clinical referral, advocacy, financial help or patient support, and we cannot arrange access to clinical trials or unapproved therapies. If you need medical help, contact your GP, specialist or local emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

This is a public signposting list for the 27 diseases covered by our current dossier library. It includes national and international organisations that publish information or support for patients and families. It is a starting point, not an exhaustive list.

No endorsement or partnership is implied. We have not been asked to list these organisations, and no organisation has reviewed this page. Links go to the organisations' own public websites; Sabre-Tek does not control their content or services.

Organisation names and links checked 16 November 2026. Websites change; if a link is out of date, use the organisation's own site search or the umbrella organisations above.
