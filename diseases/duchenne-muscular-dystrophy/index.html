02What it is

Duchenne muscular dystrophy is an X-linked dystrophinopathy caused by pathogenic variants in DMD. It is the most common childhood muscular dystrophy, but it is not one uniform disease: the exact variant, reading frame and disease stage change what a research programme can measure and which therapy is even eligible.

Dystrophin is absent or markedly reduced in DMD. Becker muscular dystrophy is the milder allelic spectrum, usually with a shortened but partially functional dystrophin. The distinction matters because a DMD diagnosis should be confirmed by genetic testing, not by clinical appearance alone.

This page describes the evidence and the research route. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, an eligibility decision or a promise of benefit. Treatment decisions belong to a qualified clinical team and the current local label.

03What goes wrong

Dystrophin links the actin cytoskeleton to the dystrophin-associated protein complex at the sarcolemma. Loss of dystrophin makes the muscle membrane more fragile during contraction, increases calcium entry and triggers inflammation, necrosis and fibrosis. The result is progressive skeletal, cardiac and respiratory disease.

The cascade is a supported mechanism, but it is not a complete causal chain. Immune responses, mitochondrial dysfunction, impaired regeneration and fibrosis can continue after partial dystrophin restoration. Restoring some dystrophin is therefore not automatically the same as restoring muscle function.

Structural note: there is no full-length experimental structure of dystrophin in this dossier. PDB 1EG3 is a dystrophin WW-domain fragment in complex with a beta-dystroglycan peptide, not the full protein. Domain fragments are useful context; they do not establish a full-length structure, a drug pocket or an in vivo effect.

04How NIGHTINGALE routes it

mechanism: structural_lof approved: restore function research: editing / utrophin not a clinical recommendation

Approved route: genotype-specific exon skipping or AAV micro-dystrophin where the label and eligibility criteria fit; corticosteroids and supportive care remain the backbone for many patients.

DMD is a loss-of-function disease: dystrophin is absent or reduced, not accumulated. Degrading residual dystrophin would be the wrong direction. No degrader candidate is proposed. The research routes are exon skipping, gene therapy, gene editing and utrophin upregulation; each is genotype-, stage- or jurisdiction-specific, and none is a universal cure.

05Treatment today

Corticosteroids

standard care in many centres
Deflazacort and prednisone/prednisolone · anti-inflammatory, not a genetic correction

Glucocorticoids slow the decline in ambulation and may delay loss of walking, but they do not restore dystrophin. The burden is substantial: weight gain, bone fragility, growth suppression, cataract, glucose intolerance and adrenal suppression. The benefit–risk balance is stage- and family-specific.

Exon-skipping antisense oligonucleotides

genotype-specific
Eteplirsen (exon 51) · golodirsen (53) · casimersen (45) · viltolarsen (53)

These products have US accelerated approvals for amenable deletions. They increase dystrophin, but the approvals rest largely on dystrophin as a surrogate. Confirmatory functional evidence is limited and inconsistent; a positive dystrophin result is not proof of functional benefit. The exact label criteria must be checked.

Delandistrogene moxeparvovec

US accelerated approval
AAVrh74 micro-dystrophin · one-time delivery

A shortened micro-dystrophin transgene, not full-length dystrophin. Immunogenicity, liver injury, cardiac events and durability are central uncertainties. The label defines the current indication, warnings and monitoring requirements; eligibility is not universal.

Givinostat and vamorolone

approved in some jurisdictions
Histone deacetylase inhibitor · dissociative steroid

These are additional options, not interchangeable cures. Givinostat carries haematological and gastrointestinal risks; vamorolone has a different steroid profile but long-term data are still developing. Jurisdiction and label status matter.

Ataluren

EMA conditional · not FDA approved
Nonsense-mutation read-through · jurisdiction-specific

Ataluren has a conditional EMA authorisation for nonsense-mutation DMD and is not FDA approved. The evidence and access position are jurisdiction-specific; this page does not present it as a general DMD treatment.

What this page does not do: it does not provide dose management, eligibility criteria or an individual treatment plan. Those require a qualified clinical team and the current local label. Approval is not the same as access, and a group result is not an individual promise.

06In the pipeline

  • Genotype-first exon skipping. The deletion, reading frame and current label determine eligibility; population percentages are not individual eligibility.
  • AAV micro-dystrophin. One-time delivery with immune, liver, cardiac and durability questions; the construct is not full-length dystrophin.
  • Gene editing. Preclinical. No clinical claim; delivery, off-target effects and durability remain unresolved.
  • Utrophin upregulation. A compensatory research route, not an approved treatment.
  • Cardiac and respiratory outcomes. These are separate domains from ambulation. A walking test does not capture cardiomyopathy or respiratory failure.
  • Access and real-world delivery. A licensed therapy may still be unavailable or inequitable; access is a separate decision layer from approval.
