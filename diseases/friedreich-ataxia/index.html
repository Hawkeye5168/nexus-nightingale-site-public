02What it is

Friedreich ataxia is the most common hereditary ataxia. Progressive gait and limb ataxia, dysarthria, and cardiomyopathy typically begin in adolescence. Most cases are caused by a GAA repeat expansion that silences frataxin, a mitochondrial protein needed for iron-sulfur cluster assembly.

03What goes wrong

Loss-of-function. The expanded GAA repeat in FXN intron 1 forms a triplex/heterochromatin structure that impedes transcription elongation and induces locus-specific transcriptional silencing of FXN. This reduces frataxin substantially — commonly cited as ~5–30% of normal — impairing mitochondrial iron-sulfur cluster (ISC) biogenesis and leading to iron accumulation and oxidative stress.

Structural note: the pipeline resolves FXN to PDB 3T3K, the Q148R disease-variant construct covering residues 82–210. It is not wild-type frataxin and not the full 210-residue precursor.

The newer FXN evidence map (7 Nov 2026) uses experimental structures 1EKG (1.8 Å) and 3S4M (1.3 Å) for its figures. They are different constructs from 3T3K and are not interchangeable; the evidence map states the construct and its limits.

04How NIGHTINGALE routes it

mechanism: enzyme_lof not a degradation target

Recommended modality: upregulator (Nrf2 activator approved) + gene therapy (investigational)

NOT eligible. FRDA is a loss-of-function disease — frataxin is depleted, not accumulated. Degrading frataxin further would worsen the disease. Correct route is frataxin restoration (upregulators, gene therapy), not protein degradation.

05Treatment today

Omaveloxolone (Skyclarys)

2023-02-28 (Reata Pharmaceuticals, NDA 216718)
Nrf2 activator · 554.7 Da · PubChem CID 71811910

Activates the Nrf2 antioxidant pathway, reducing oxidative stress and improving mitochondrial function. It does NOT restore frataxin expression directly; it mitigates downstream oxidative damage.

UK access: The MHRA approved omaveloxolone on 23 April 2025. NICE TA1061 was terminated on 6 May 2025 after Biogen withdrew its submission, and SMC2845 did not recommend it on 9 March 2026. It is not routinely available on the NHS; individual funding routes exist but are discretionary.

Trial evidence: MOXIe part 2 (NCT02255435) randomised 103 people; 82 were in the full analysis set. The published 48-week result reports a between-group mFARS difference of −2.40 ± 0.96 (p = 0.014), with transient aminotransferase increases and more headache, nausea and fatigue in the omaveloxolone arm (Lynch et al., Ann Neurol 2021). This is a group result, not a guarantee of individual or long-term benefit.

06In the pipeline

  • Frataxin gene therapy (AAV-mediated, preclinical/early clinical)
  • Etravirine (NNRTI) — reported to increase frataxin expression (Phase 2 FAEST1, NCT04273165, COMPLETED Jan 2023)
  • Omaveloxolone + idebenone (Nrf2 + mitochondrial antioxidant)
  • Histone deacetylase inhibitors (e.g. HDAC3) — preclinical frataxin upregulation

Key reference (frataxin maturation): PMID 18725397 · PubMed
