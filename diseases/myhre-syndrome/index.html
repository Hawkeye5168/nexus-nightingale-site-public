02What it is

Myhre syndrome is a rare autosomal dominant SMAD4-related disorder with growth, cardiac, respiratory, joint, skin and developmental features.

The clinical spectrum is broad and the natural history is incompletely characterised. Diagnosis is genetic, with a recognized pathogenic variant.

This page is a research summary. It is not a diagnosis, an eligibility decision or a treatment recommendation.

03What goes wrong

SMAD4 is a TGF-beta/BMP signalling effector. The disease is not simply loss of tumour suppression; the relevant mechanism is developmental signalling dysregulation.

Construct limit: PDB 1DD1 is the SMAD4 active fragment, not full-length SMAD4. A near-perfect template-recovery score is not a blind prediction and not a full-length structural model.

05Treatment today

There is no approved disease-modifying therapy. Cardiac, respiratory, joint and developmental surveillance with symptom-directed care is the baseline.

The page does not provide dosing, eligibility or an individual treatment plan. Those belong to a qualified clinical team and the current local label.
