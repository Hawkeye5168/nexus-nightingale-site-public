02What it is

Children with HGPS age ~8x faster than normal. Growth failure, hair loss, skin changes, and premature atherosclerosis lead to death from heart attack or stroke at a median age of ~14.5 years.

03What goes wrong

Gain-of-function / toxic protein. The c.1824C>T (p.Gly608Gly) variant activates a cryptic splice donor in LMNA exon 11, causing an in-frame deletion of 50 codons of prelamin A. The resulting isoform 'progerin' retains its C-terminal CAAX box and is farnesylated, but lacks the ZMPSTE24 cleavage site (which lies within the deleted region), so it remains permanently farnesylated and anchors at the nuclear lamina, disrupting nuclear architecture.

04How NIGHTINGALE routes it

mechanism: toxic_gof AUTOTAC-eligible

Recommended modality: degrader (selective progerin) + farnesyltransferase inhibitor (approved)

Progerin is a toxic gain-of-function protein; selective degradation is mechanistically appropriate (in principle). NOTE: the approved drug lonafarnib is NOT an AUTOTAC — see treatment.

05Treatment today

Lonafarnib (Zokinvy)

2020-11-20 (Eiger BioPharmaceuticals)
Farnesyltransferase inhibitor · 638.8 Da · PubChem CID 148195

Inhibits farnesyltransferase, blocking the farnesylation step required for progerin to anchor at the nuclear envelope — thereby reducing toxic progerin accumulation. It does NOT bind LMNA/progerin directly.

06In the pipeline

  • CRISPR base editing to correct the c.1824C>T splice variant (preclinical)
  • Progerin-targeting PROTAC/AUTOTAC degraders (investigational, not yet clinical)

Combination studied: Lonafarnib + pravastatin + zoledronic acid (farnesylation inhibitor triple, Circulation 2016, PMID 27400896)

Key reference: PMID 27400896 · PubMed
