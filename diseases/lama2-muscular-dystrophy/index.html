LAMA2-related congenital muscular dystrophy (MDC1A)

A source-linked research dossier on LAMA2-related loss of function. LAMA2-001 v1.0 is not clinical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation or a regulatory submission.

01What the dossier is

LAMA2-001 is a decision-grade research dossier built from public reference data, disease-specific literature and a recorded data preflight. It follows the Sabre-Tek Dossier Benchmark v1: every section carries a key finding, an analysis and a falsifier; every claim is source-linked; the source register is printed once.

02Disease and mechanism

The dossier is anchored to LAMA2 (UniProt P24043) and the inheritance pattern autosomal recessive. The recorded mechanism is loss of laminin-211 at the sarcolemma with congenital muscular dystrophy.

The mechanism gate is BLOCKED — loss of function; no degrader. A degradation strategy would remove a protein the patient already lacks. Restoration, replacement, compensation or supportive care are the only directions consistent with the mechanism; none is presented here as an approved disease-modifying treatment.

03Approved care and research boundary

Current standard care is respiratory, nutritional, mobility and orthopaedic support. The dossier separates approved/standard care from investigational work; approval is not the same as access, and a standard treatment is not necessarily a cure.

The leading research route is LAMA2 gene replacement and laminin-restoration strategies are investigational. It is included because it is the most plausible next step, not because it has established benefit. Monitoring covers motor function, respiratory function, feeding and growth, contractures and scoliosis, seizures.

04Data preflight and source check

Before the dossier was written, the data preflight checked 81 source nodes across identity, structure, variation, expression, pathways, trials, literature, countermeasures and diagnostics: 49 PASS, 30 PARTIAL, 2 BLOCKED. No deep MSA was available for this target, so the dossier makes no DCA or ContactNet claim. Source records were admitted as SCOUTED records; they are not clinical claims.

The source register uses stable destinations: UniProt, Orphanet, OMIM/MedGen, PubMed articles, ClinicalTrials.gov and public reference archives. Live pages can change; the dossier records the date of the source check. No patient data were used.

05What is not claimed

  • No approved disease-modifying treatment and no cure.
  • No blind structural prediction: no experimental full-length structure was found, and no fold score is presented as a structure.
  • No DCA contact map: no deep MSA was available for this target.
  • No independent clinical review has been completed.
  • No claim that a patient will respond to any research route.
