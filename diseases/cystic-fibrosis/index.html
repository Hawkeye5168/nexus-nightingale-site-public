02What it is

Cystic fibrosis is an autosomal recessive disorder caused by CFTR mutations, most commonly F508del, which misfolds the chloride channel and prevents it reaching the cell surface. It causes progressive lung disease, pancreatic insufficiency, and male infertility. CFTR modulator drugs (correctors + potentiators) rescue the protein's folding and gating — a fundamentally different strategy from degradation or enzyme replacement.

03What goes wrong

Trafficking defect + channel gating defect. The F508del mutation causes CFTR to misfold and be retained in the endoplasmic reticulum (a trafficking/folding defect), so insufficient chloride channels reach the cell surface. This is NOT a simple loss-of-function (some protein is made) nor a gain-of-function — it is a protein-folding/trafficking defect that also impairs channel gating.

04How NIGHTINGALE routes it

mechanism: trafficking_defect not a degradation target

Recommended modality: corrector (lumacaftor/elexacaftor — rescue folding) + potentiator (ivacaftor — restore gating)

NOT eligible. CF is a trafficking/folding defect, not a toxic aggregate or a simple enzyme deficiency. Degrading CFTR would worsen the already-insufficient channel function. The correct route is small-molecule correctors (rescue folding to surface) + potentiators (restore gating) — the CFTR modulator class.

05Treatment today

Ivacaftor (Kalydeco) + elexacaftor/tezacaftor (Trikafta)

2012 (ivacaftor); 2019 (Trikafta triple combination)
CFTR potentiator (ivacaftor) + corrector (elexacaftor/tezacaftor) · 392.5 Da · PubChem CID 16220172

Ivacaftor potentiates channel gating of surface CFTR; elexacaftor/tezacaftor correct F508del folding/trafficking to the surface. Together they restore CFTR function. This is a protein-rescue strategy, not degradation or replacement.

06In the pipeline

  • CFTR gene therapy (AAV/lentiviral, clinical)
  • mRNA-based CFTR delivery (investigational)
  • Readthrough agents (ataluren) for nonsense CFTR mutations
