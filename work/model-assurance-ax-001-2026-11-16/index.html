Model assurance sample · AX-001 · 16 November 2026

EEG seizure classifier

A subject-held-out demonstrator at fixed false-positive budgets. Real result, null control, stated limits — and a recommendation not to deploy yet.

Public-data demonstrator, not clinical validation. 500 subjects from the UCI Epileptic Seizure Recognition dataset; no patient data; no clinical review.

Null control: AUC 0.504 and TPR 1% at the 1% budget — the result collapses when labels are permuted. That is what makes the real result believable. The full pack also reports per-background-class false positives, leakage checks and the limits.

Open the 11-page pack PDF →Model assurance packs →

Decision

Not ready for a client pilot. At a 1% false-positive budget the classifier detects 11%; at 10%, 25%. The next step is an external cohort, a stronger model and a pre-registered operating point — not deployment.

Not a clinical claim. This is a public-data methods demonstration. It is not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, regulatory submission or evidence of patient benefit.

Source

UCI Machine Learning Repository — Epileptic Seizure Recognition, DOI 10.24432/C5G308. Dataset checksum and the full source register are in the pack.
