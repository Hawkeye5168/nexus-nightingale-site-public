What this check is

Each dossier was treated as a set of separate claims, not one pass/fail document. On 16 November 2026 we checked:

  • every active dossier against the Sabre-Tek Dossier 2.0 benchmark audit;
  • the UniProt identity source for every dossier;
  • every unique source URL in the active dossiers, with query-style URLs and dead links recorded separately;
  • page count, section count, source count and claim count from the current rendered products.

“Checked” means the named source was resolved on the date shown, or the audit rule passed. It does not mean the disease claim is clinically validated. The page is a provenance record, not a clinical guideline.

What this does not establish

Not clinical validation. This pass does not establish efficacy, safety, prevalence, natural history, regulatory approval, availability or patient benefit. External clinical review is 0/27. Foundation or patient-priority review is 0/27. A complete NICE/SMC/EMA/local access assessment is outstanding; PRCA-001 records two NICE technology appraisals as a partial check.
  • No patient data or patient contact is included.
  • No foundation is described as a partner or endorser. The public foundations directory is signposting only.
  • Source content was not fully re-read for every citation; the link and identity checks are the stated boundary.
  • 403, 429 and PubMed 203 responses are access barriers, not proof that the source is wrong.
