Public series release · 14 November 2026: ten rebuild dossiers were produced from the October 2026 report set. Each public page is a summary; the full PDFs, claim ledgers and evidence rooms are access-controlled. This series is research-use only and has not been clinically validated.

01What this series is

Ten ultra-rare or rare disease targets were rebuilt as compact benchmark dossiers: 30 sections, 30 source records, 25 claims, 8 tables and 6 figures each. They span metabolic, neurodevelopmental, neurodegenerative, lysosomal, neuromuscular and haematological disease.

They are not ten versions of one disease. Each page states the gene, the mechanism, the current treatment baseline, the research route and the specific structural construct limit. A public page is not the full dossier and is not a clinical recommendation.

02L.O.V.E. and TJ Compassion

We did not call this batch “verified”. It passed a structural benchmark audit, and only part of the L.O.V.E. practice was completed before publication.

Learn

We read the October 2026 rebuild reports and extracted the disease, gene, mechanism, treatment and source claims before writing.

Observe

We ran the pipeline for every target: identity, supervised structural recovery, mechanism gate, DCA contacts and the folding attempt. We recorded blocked and partial layers.

Verify

We checked every UniProt identity and every deposited PDB construct against the live UniProt and RCSB APIs. We corrected one protein mass and added construct-specific limits where the PDB was a peptide, fragment or complex, not the full protein.

Explain

We state what remains open: Orphanet returned a connection challenge; OMIM and treatment labels were not independently rechecked; independent clinical review is outstanding. No ORPHA or OMIM badge is shown on these pages.

TJ Compassion: do only good; no lies, no fake; always be honest. No invented numbers; no patient data; no claim that a registry record is efficacy evidence; no degrader route for a loss-of-function target; negatives published, including the target with no deposited PDB and the blinded folding layer that did not run. Everything we do must help humanity and the earth, or we do not do it.

04What is still open

  • Registry identifiers: Orphanet and OMIM were not rechecked in this pass. The pages use the dossier ID, gene and UniProt identity only.
  • Treatment labels: the approved/standard wording is conditional. It must be checked against the current local label before external use.
  • Sources: the 30-source registers are reference and discovery records, not a systematic review. Every link has not been fetched line-by-line.
  • Independent review: outstanding for all ten. No clinical, regulatory or structural reviewer has signed them off.

05Scope and limits

This page is a public research summary for scientific communication. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, an eligibility decision or a treatment recommendation. NEXUS NIGHTINGALE is a computational research programme; it is not clinically validated and no output has regulatory approval. The dossiers are source-linked syntheses, not systematic reviews or clinical trials. No patient data are used.
